Kidnapped I

Mary Kilpatrick  ·  Original Figurative Painting

MediumAcrylic, Mixed Media
Dimensions8″ × 8″
AvailabilityAvailable
Price$3,000

Kidnapped I is an original figurative work by Mary Kilpatrick, painted in 2023, in acrylic, mixed media on a 8 in by 8 in canvas. The palette is balanced and richly coloured, reading bright and high-key overall, which is what informs the frame and mat pairing recommended on the prints page. Kilpatrick's figurative work draws on a career on the Broadway stage, and the paintings carry that same attention to gesture, presence, and the weight a figure holds in a room.
