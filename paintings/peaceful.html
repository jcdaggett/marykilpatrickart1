Peaceful

Mary Kilpatrick  ·  Original Figurative Painting

MediumAcrylic on canvas
Dimensions22 × 28 in
AvailabilityAvailable
Price$15,000

Not a conventional portrait called Peaceful — an interrogation of it. In Peaceful, Kilpatrick explores what we project onto women's bodies and what women know themselves to be. The result is a painting that is visually arresting and emotionally complex — a work that earns its title by refusing to simplify it.
