Fantasy Land is an original figurative work by Mary Kilpatrick, painted in 2010, in acrylic on a 18 in by 24 in canvas. The palette is warm and restrained in colour, reading bright and high-key overall, which is what informs the frame and mat pairing recommended on the prints page. Kilpatrick's figurative work draws on a career on the Broadway stage, and the paintings carry that same attention to gesture, presence, and the weight a figure holds in a room.
