Las Vegas

Mary Kilpatrick  ·  Original Figurative Painting

MediumAcrylic on canvas
AvailabilityAvailable
Price$3,500

A showgirl figure rendered with bold theatrical energy — back turned, adorned, commanding. The composition draws directly from Kilpatrick's years performing in theater, where the body becomes a statement before a single word is spoken. The painting celebrates the performer's craft: the discipline, the spectacle, and the power of a woman who owns the stage.
