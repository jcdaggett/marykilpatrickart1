Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through this website, we collect your name, email address, and any information you include in your message. When you purchase a print, we also collect your shipping address and payment information. Payment card data is processed by Stripe and is never stored on our servers.

We use your information solely to respond to your inquiries and fulfill print orders. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This site uses Stripe for payment processing, Lumaprints for print fulfillment, and Resend for email delivery. Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Google Analytics is used to understand site traffic in aggregate.

Data Retention

Inquiry data is retained for the purpose of responding to and following up on your request. Order data is retained as required for financial and legal compliance.
