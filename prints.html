Before You Order

How long does production and shipping take?

Your print is produced in 2–3 business days and ships via USPS, FedEx, or UPS. Allow 7–10 business days total from order to delivery. You'll receive a tracking notification when your order ships.

How will my print be packaged?

Prints 8×10″ and smaller ship in rigid stay-flat cardboard envelopes. Larger prints ship in kraft mailing tubes. Every print is carefully packaged to protect against moisture and impact in transit.

How long will the print last?

The archival pigment inks and 100% cotton rag paper used in every print are guaranteed to last over 100 years. These are the same materials used in museum and gallery editions worldwide — built to outlast the walls they hang on.

How should I display and care for my print?

Keep your print away from direct sunlight — UV exposure is the primary cause of color fading over time. Frame behind optical-grade clear acrylic or glass for long-term protection. Dust with a soft dry cloth only.

What if my print arrives damaged?

Contact us at art@marykilpatrick.com with photos of the damage and packaging. A replacement will be sent as soon as possible. Every print carries a lifetime quality guarantee — if there's a defect, we make it right.

What is the return policy?

Every print carries a lifetime guarantee for quality and workmanship. If your print has a defect, we will send a replacement or issue a refund. Because these are custom limited edition prints produced to order, we cannot accept returns for change of mind — please choose carefully.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship to most countries worldwide via USPS international. Shipping is calculated at checkout. Please note that customs duties and taxes are the buyer's responsibility and vary by country. We are unable to ship to Germany at this time. PO Box delivery available for smaller prints.

Can I request a different size?

Each print is offered at the original painting's dimensions. If you need a different size, contact us and we'll do our best to accommodate your request. Custom sizes are available on inquiry.
