Acquiring an Original

Every original is one of a kind.
Here's how it gets to you safely.

Each painting is acrylic on canvas. Several works incorporate real feathers, gemstones, and found materials physically embedded in the surface — making those originals truly three-dimensional objects. Below is exactly how the process works, what protects you, and what to expect from inquiry to delivery.

Interested in a painting?

Tell us which painting and we'll be in touch within one business day.

Secure Payment

Payment is processed via a secure link — all major credit cards accepted. You have full buyer protection and recourse if anything goes wrong. No wire transfers, no cash.

Insured & Tracked Shipping

Every original ships via FedEx or UPS with declared value coverage and full tracking. You'll receive tracking the day it ships and can watch it move. Every painting is packed with acid-free tissue, bubble wrap, and rigid crating — the same standard used for gallery shipments.

Certificate of Authenticity

Every original ships with a signed Certificate of Authenticity documenting the title, medium, dimensions, and date. This travels with the painting and establishes its provenance — important for resale, insurance, and estate purposes.

Pre-Ship Documentation

Before your painting ships, it's photographed in detail — front, back, edges, any mixed media elements — and those images are sent to you. You know exactly what you're receiving before it leaves us. No surprises on arrival.

Damage Protection

If your painting arrives damaged in transit, we make it right — period. Photograph the damage before opening the packaging fully, contact us within 48 hours, and we handle the claim and resolution. You will not be left holding a damaged painting.

A Real Person to Call

This isn't a faceless e-commerce operation. We can be reached directly at (458) 666-3614 or art@marykilpatrick.com. Questions before you buy are welcome. So are questions after.

How it works

1

Send an inquiry

Email art@marykilpatrick.com or call (458) 666-3614 with the painting you're interested in. Include any questions — size, what it looks like in person, whether it's framed, anything.

Response within one business day. Usually sooner.

2

Confirm availability & details

We'll confirm availability, answer your questions, and share any additional detail photos you'd like — specific angles, texture close-ups, how it looks under different lighting.

Paintings are held for 48 hours during active inquiry — not indefinitely. First to complete payment secures the work.

3

Secure payment

You'll receive a secure invoice link by email. All major credit cards accepted, with full buyer protection. Payment in full secures the work — no deposits, no payment plans.

Accepted: all major credit cards. Buyer protection included.

4

Pre-ship photos & packing

Before anything moves, the painting is photographed in full and those images are sent to you. It's then packed using gallery-standard materials — acid-free tissue, archival padding, bubble wrap, and a rigid outer crate appropriate for the size.

Packing typically takes 1–2 days. You'll be notified when it ships.

5

Tracked, insured delivery

Ships via FedEx or UPS with declared value coverage and full tracking. You receive the tracking number the day it ships. Estimated transit 3–7 business days within the continental US. Signature required on delivery.

Currently shipping within the continental United States only.

6

Your painting arrives

Unpack carefully, keeping all packaging materials until you've confirmed the condition. Inside the crate you'll find the painting and its Certificate of Authenticity. If anything looks off, photograph it immediately and contact us within 48 hours.

Buyer is responsible for shipping cost. Exact shipping is calculated based on size and destination and included on your invoice — no markup.

Payment

Payment in full required before shipping. All major credit cards accepted via secure invoice link. No wire transfers.

Shipping

Continental US only. FedEx or UPS, declared value coverage, full tracking. Actual cost billed at cost — no markup. Signature required on delivery.

Returns & Damage

All sales are final. Damage in transit is covered: photograph immediately, contact us within 48 hours, and we handle it.
